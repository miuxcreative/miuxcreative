Case file 04RoyaFit · 2016

Make success your only option.

Branding and digital experiences for Miami’s first fitness concierge.

RoyaFit launched in 2016 as Miami’s first fitness concierge, bringing elite classes and personal training to hotels, luxury residences and private clients. It was the first MIUX chapter with Roya, years before its sister studio, RF Studios, opened its doors.

Brutally simple

The wall

The site, as it lived

White as the canvas, lime as the only color, and one slanted button on every page. One shape, used everywhere, made it feel designed instead of assembled.
