Case file 03RF Studios

Luxury, lift, and a lot of air time.

Branding and digital experiences for a South Beach aerial fitness studio.

After years building RoyaFit, Miami’s fitness concierge, Roya came back with its sister: a luxury aerial studio in South Beach. MIUX built it from the ground (and ceiling) up: strategy, an Art Deco identity rooted in Miami, the space and the website, so the whole place feels elevated, grounded and alive.

Where it began

Once known as RoyaFit, the brand was ready for its next chapter. As it moved into a physical space, MIUX rebuilt the identity to match its energy, elegance and lift.

Design in motion

MIUX explored abstract, symbolic directions before landing on Art Deco: bold, structured and rooted in Miami. The final system puts motion and meaning in every curve.

Every detail counts
